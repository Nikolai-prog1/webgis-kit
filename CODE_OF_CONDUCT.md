# Community conduct

This project adapts [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), credited to the Contributor Covenant community under CC BY 4.0.

Participation should be welcoming across identities, backgrounds, abilities and experience. Discuss ideas respectfully, consider feedback and take responsibility for harm. Harassment, sexual pressure, discriminatory remarks, threats, insults, disruptive targeting and exposure of private information are unacceptable. These expectations cover project spaces and people acting as project representatives.

Maintainers can moderate contributions and explain decisions when appropriate. Raise ordinary concerns through GitHub issues. For sensitive reports, use GitHub private vulnerability reporting when available; otherwise request a private channel without posting personal details. Reports should be handled discreetly and fairly, including affected people's safety and privacy.

Responses should fit the behavior and its impact: corrective feedback for isolated mistakes, a formal warning for repeated disruption, a temporary restriction for serious misconduct, or removal from participation for persistent or severe harm. Maintainers are subject to the same standards. Decisions should consider context, communicate applicable boundaries and avoid public disclosure of a reporter's identity.
